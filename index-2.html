📈 Progressive overload

Start at the lower end of each rep range. Add 1–2 reps when every set is clean. Once every set reaches the top of its range, choose a harder variation or a small increase in difficulty. Don't add reps, sets and difficulty all at once. Rest 90–120 seconds for demanding exercises and 45–60 seconds for smaller movements.

🗓️ 12-week campaign

Weeks 1–3 · Foundation

Learn technique, establish baseline numbers, and build consistency.

Weeks 4–6 · Strength

Gradually add reps and improve pull-up and push-up quality.

Weeks 7–9 · Muscle-building

Progress to harder variations only when the current range is mastered.

Weeks 10–12 · Performance

Retest clean reps and compare measurements/photos. Reduce volume before testing if tired.

❤️ Cardio & recovery

Walking: 20–30 minutes on 5–6 days, at a comfortable pace. Skipping: 5–10 minutes on up to 2 days if joints feel good. Saturday: finish with 8–10 minutes easy skipping or brisk walking. Sunday: rest, or relaxed walking and gentle mobility.

Recovery: Aim for 7–9 hours of sleep, eat enough to support training, and include affordable protein sources when available.

Pull-ups and chin-ups require a securely installed bar. Never use an unstable door, table or furniture. If you cannot access a safe bar, skip those movements rather than improvising; bodyweight-only back training is limited without a safe pulling setup. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness or feeling unwell.
