Training protocol

Beginner-friendly home routine. Use a stable surface and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or unusual symptoms.

Day A • Full body20–30 MIN
Incline or wall push-ups

2 sets × 6–12 reps. Choose a height that lets you keep good form.

Bodyweight squats

2 sets × 8–12 reps. Use a comfortable depth.

Glute bridges

2 sets × 10–15 reps.

Bird-dog

2 sets × 6 each side, controlled.

Easy walk

5–10 minutes to finish or later in the day.

Day B • Full body20–30 MIN
Incline push-ups

2 sets × 6–12 reps.

Reverse lunges

2 sets × 6–10 each side; hold support if needed.

Dead bug

2 sets × 8 each side.

Calf raises

2 sets × 12–15 reps.

Gentle mobility

5 minutes.

Alternate A and B with rest or easy walking between strength sessions. Start with 2–3 strength days per week; progress gradually.
